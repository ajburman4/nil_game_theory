# Model status: no fitted model

As of October 17, 2026, this repository contains an initial financial inventory
and a gated research notebook. No empirical response coefficients, causal NIL
effects, athlete-retention response, expected win predictions, optimized school
allocations or equilibrium have been estimated.

**Intended use:** transparent research and conditional athletics resource
scenarios, after coverage and validation gates pass. The output is not athlete
contract advice or a legal compliance determination.

**Current data:** official EADA archives, 2017–18 through 2024–25, with public DI
reporting-year classifications. Institutional disclosures are observed as
disclosures, not independently audited truth. Performance, source-date mapping,
inflation and year-specific peer membership have not been joined.

**Planned baselines:** prior-season and peer means, then separate sport panel
models comparing absolute/peer-relative resources and linear/log/spline forms.
All coaching proxies, preprocessing and normalizations must use training history
only. Final validation seasons will be frozen after actual joined coverage is
known and before fitting. See config/research_plan.json.

**Planned simulations:** generic profiles with clearly labeled funding, utility
weights and response assumptions; protected other-program commitments and a
reserve option. Separate cash, institutional payments, counted benefits,
external NIL, coaching and operations. Any equilibrium result needs independently
checked unilateral regret and stated grid/search accuracy.

**Current limitations:** missing outcomes and institution-demand data; possible
finance definition changes and availability leakage; campus/sport membership
verification; unresolved redistribution permissions; incomplete current policy
verification. These prevent historical inference and operational policy modes.
No named school has been assigned an illustrative NIL tier or objective.
