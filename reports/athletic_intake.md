# Optional Athletic screenshot intake

The project proceeds without this source. If the owner supplies screenshots
from lawful access, place them in `data/local/restricted/athletic/`.

Useful screenshots show the original title and URL, publication/update date,
methodology and legend, dollar units, season, school names and reported ranges.
Include any definition explaining whether figures represent institutional
payments, external NIL, combined roster resources, planned capacity, commitments
or cash paid. A screenshot without the legend may be impossible to classify.
Partial coverage is acceptable; no need to reconstruct the whole interactive.

For each supplied file record its checksum, source, capture date and exact scope
in a local intake manifest. Transcribe only visible values; preserve bounds,
unknowns and source caveats. Label values `reported_estimate`, with manual review
against the screenshot. A midpoint is a scenario convenience, not an observation.
Football estimates stay football-only. Do not subtract guessed institutional
payments to manufacture external NIL. Do not redistribute screenshots, article
text or a reconstructed proprietary table. Derived public use needs a documented
permission/rights decision first; otherwise provide acquisition instructions.
